## If a Cancellation Does Happen: Tickets, Travel, and Refunds

Planning ahead costs nothing and protects you if the situation ever changes. The table below is a general playbook for festival cancellations, and it applies whether the event in question is GoldenSky or any other large outdoor show.

| Timing | What to do | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Before you buy | Use a credit card and save the confirmation email | Gives you a paper trail and chargeback options |
| First 24 hours of a rumor | Do nothing except check official channels | Panic-selling tickets is how people lose money |
| When an official notice lands | Follow the stated refund process exactly | Third-party "refund helpers" cannot speed it up |
| For flights and hotels | Review your cancellation windows immediately | Many bookings are flexible up to 24–48 hours out |
| For resale purchases | Contact the resale platform, not the stranger who sold to you | Refunds typically route to the original purchaser |

Two practical tips worth repeating. First, never pay a stranger who DMs you offering to "process" a festival refund — that is one of the oldest scams in live music. Second, screenshot your order confirmation and store it somewhere searchable. When thousands of fans email a box office at once, the person with an order number gets helped first.
