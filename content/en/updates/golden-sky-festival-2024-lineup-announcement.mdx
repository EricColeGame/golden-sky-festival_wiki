## Anatomy of a GoldenSky Lineup Drop

GoldenSky doesn't dump its lineup without warning. The festival runs a predictable rollout that rewards fans who pay attention early, and the pattern has stayed consistent across announcement cycles. The festival's own social channels have promoted a sign-up link for "first access to the lineup announcement and presale," with the actual reveal landing on a Thursday morning at 10 a.m. Pacific.

If you understand that sequence, you stop scrambling. Here is the general shape of a GoldenSky announcement cycle:

| Phase | What happens | What you should do |
|---|---|---|
| Teaser | Social posts hint at a date and push a sign-up link | Join the email/SMS list immediately |
| Lineup drop | Full bill revealed, typically a Thursday at 10 a.m. PT | Screenshot it; check day-by-day splits |
| Presale | Registered fans get first buying window | Have payment info ready before the window opens |
| General on sale | Remaining tickets released | Compare single-day vs. weekend pricing |

The presale step is the one most people skip. Signing up costs nothing and routinely determines whether you get a face-value ticket or end up on resale sites.
