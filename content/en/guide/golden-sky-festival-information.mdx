## First-Timer Tips and What to Pack

This is the part of any Golden Sky Festival information guide that pays off most on the day itself. Country festival crowds are generally friendly and relaxed, but a two-day outdoor event still rewards preparation.

**Practical tips that actually help:**

- **Hydrate before you are thirsty.** Water refill stations usually have shorter lines than bars.
- **Wear broken-in boots or sneakers.** The grounds are grass and dirt, and you will walk several miles a day.
- **Bring a light layer for the evening.** A packable jacket beats buying merch because you got cold.
- **Screenshot the schedule.** You will not want to rely on signal in a crowd.
- **Set a daytime meetup.** Sunset is when groups get separated.
- **Respect the bag policy.** Clear-bag rules are common and enforcement is at the gate, not inside.

| Category | Pack | Skip |
|---|---|---|
| Clothing | Hat, sunglasses, light jacket, comfortable shoes | Heavy layers, new boots |
| Tech | Charged phone, portable battery | Drones, professional cameras |
| Comfort | Earplugs, sunscreen, bandana | Glass containers, outside alcohol |
| Documents | ID, pass screenshot, parking confirmation | Anything you cannot replace |

First-timers also tend to over-plan. Pick two or three acts you refuse to miss per day, then let the rest of the schedule happen. That is the difference between a fun weekend and a forced march between stages.
