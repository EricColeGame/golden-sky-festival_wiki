## Where the Golden Sky Festival Map Puts You: Discovery Park Basics

Discovery Park is a 302-acre riverfront property sitting right against Interstate 5 in downtown Sacramento. The park's shape is defined by water on two sides, which is the most important orientation cue on any Golden Sky Festival map: rivers form the edges of the site, and the entrances sit on the north and south approaches.

Because the grounds are large and mostly open, the map is less about finding a building and more about understanding flow — where you come in, how far you are from the stage areas, and how long it takes to cross from one side to the other. Plan on more walking than you would at an arena show.

One practical consequence of the geography: rideshare drop-offs and pedestrian routes funnel toward two entrances only. Pick your entrance before you leave the hotel, not after you are standing in traffic on Garden Highway.

## Planning Around the Map: Gates, Timing, and Re-Entry

The map only solves half the problem. The other half is timing, and GoldenSky has a strict policy that catches first-timers off guard: re-entry is not permitted. Once you are inside for the day, you stay inside — unless you hold a Camping pass, a Country Club SVIP pass, or an Admission + Hotel package booked through Jampack.

That single rule reshapes how you should use the map. Everything you need for a full day — layers, sunscreen, your one sealed water bottle, a light blanket — has to come in with you on the first pass through the gate.

| Timeframe | What to Do |
| --- | --- |
| Months out | Buy passes, book lodging, decide which entrance fits your route |
| Two weeks out | Download the set-times schedule and save the map to your phone |
| Night before | Check the weather, pack to the allowed-items list, confirm your entry point |
| Morning of | Arrive ahead of the noon-1 p.m. gate window and expect a security line |
| Mid-day | Locate the Info Booth, lockers, and medical tent while crowds are lighter |
| Evening | Agree on a meeting point near your exit entrance before phones start dying |

## What the Map's Security Lanes Will Turn Away

Every entrance has a screening lane, and the fastest way to clog it is to arrive with something prohibited. GoldenSky actively discourages bringing bags at all; if you must bring one, follow the posted bag policy.

| Allowed | Not Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Cell phones and small portable chargers | Large bags, backpacks, and duffle bags |
| Non-aerosol sunscreen | Outside food and beverages (one sealed 20 oz water is the exception) |
| One factory-sealed water bottle (20 oz max) or one empty plastic refillable bottle | Metal containers such as Yetis or Hydroflasks |
| Binoculars, earplugs, hats, sunglasses | Chairs of any kind, including inflatable loungers |
| Towels and light blankets (restricted in some areas) | Umbrellas, strollers, coolers, picnic baskets |
| Small point-and-shoot cameras | Professional cameras, GoPros, selfie sticks, drones |
| Sealed packs of cigarettes and cigars, vape pens | Weapons, drugs, fireworks, lasers, noisemakers, pets |

Guests with dietary needs can bring small amounts of food in clear zip-top bags, plus a soft-sided cooler no larger than 12" x 9" x 9", and should use the Access Lane. Prescriptions are permitted when the name on the bottle matches a valid ID, limited to a one-day supply.

## Using the Golden Sky Festival Map on Your Phone

The festival's official app is the companion piece to the printed map. It carries the schedule, lets you build a personal plan, and pushes updates when something shifts — which, at an outdoor event, it sometimes does.

A few habits that pay off:

- Download the app and the map image before you leave Wi-Fi.
- Drop a pin on your entrance and your exit so you are not guessing at 11 p.m.
- Pick a physical meeting point near a labeled landmark — a specific booth beats "by the stage."
- Bring a small portable charger; navigation and photos drain batteries fast.
- Check the app for weather pauses. The festival is rain or shine but can pause or evacuate for lightning, high wind, hail, or flooding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where can I find the Golden Sky Festival map?**
The official map is published in the Festival Map section of the festival's info page, alongside the venue address and general FAQs. Save a copy to your phone before the event, since connectivity inside the park can be inconsistent.

**Which entrance should I use at Discovery Park?**
There are two: the Garden Highway entrance on the north side of the park and the Jibboom Street entrance from the south. Choose the one that matches your route into Sacramento — guests arriving via I-5 from the north generally favor Garden Highway, while downtown and south-side arrivals often prefer Jibboom Street.

**Can I leave and come back later in the day?**
No. Re-entry is not permitted unless you have a Camping pass, a Country Club SVIP pass, or an Admission + Hotel package booked through Jampack. Pack for the entire day before you go through security.

**Are chairs allowed on the festival grounds?**
Outside chairs are not permitted, so the map's seating and activation areas matter more than usual. Light blankets and towels are allowed, though blankets are restricted in certain areas for safety reasons.
