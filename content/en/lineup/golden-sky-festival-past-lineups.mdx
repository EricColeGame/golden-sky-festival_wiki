## Build Your Own Lineup Archive in Five Steps

1. **Start with the official channels.** Scroll back through the festival's posts and save every lineup announcement you find.
2. **Capture full-resolution images.** Zoom in before saving — tiering is easiest to read on a clean file.
3. **Cross-check dates.** A poster without a year is nearly useless two editions later.
4. **Log set times if you can find them.** Even approximate set times reveal how long headliners actually play.
5. **Note your own reactions.** Write one line about each act you saw. In three years, that's the most valuable record you'll have.
