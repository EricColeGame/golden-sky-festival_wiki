## Why Sacramento Bet on GoldenSky's Return

The festival's comeback isn't just a fan-service story. Past editions generated roughly $15 million a year in spending from visitors patronizing Sacramento hotels, restaurants and bars, according to organizers speaking with CapRadio. That figure is why the city, the county and Visit Sacramento remain partners in the event.

GoldenSky is produced by Danny Wimmer Presents in partnership with [Visit Sacramento](https://www.visitsacramento.com/), the city and the county — and the new deal commits the festival to Sacramento through 2029. In other words, the two-year pause wasn't a wind-down. It was a reset.

Organizers have also framed the 2027 edition as a broadening rather than a rebrand. Country remains the heart of the weekend, but adding an Americana headliner and a long list of emerging acts gives the festival room to grow beyond the audience that showed up for the original Golden Sky Festival 2023 lineup announcement cycle.
